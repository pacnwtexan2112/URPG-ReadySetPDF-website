Prepress software FAQ

Straight answers for shop owners comparing local automation to cloud PDF tools.

What is ReadySetPDF?

ReadySetPDF is Windows prepress automation from ReadySetPDF LLC. Hot folders, variable data, imposition, fold proofs, and preflight run on your own hardware. Customer files stay in the shop.

Is ReadySetPDF free to download?

Version 3.54 is the current free Windows download. 4.0, coming soon, starts with a 30-day trial, then Free or Pro. Free keeps Resize, Crop, Rotate, Cutter, Unlock, Folds, and the on-screen preflight report. Pro adds mail merge, color, automation, OCR, booklet, Monkey, Grid, and preflight export plus file routing.

Does ReadySetPDF work offline?

Yes. Files never upload to a browser or a cloud queue. That means no file-size caps, no wait on a shared server, and no PHI or customer lists leaving the shop — which matters for healthcare mail and other regulated work.

What is a prepress hot folder?

A watched folder. In 4.0, three pipelines each have their own input, output, and saved recipe. Drop a PDF and the recipe runs. Drop CSV, Excel, or ODS onto a recipe that already holds the envelope, and it mail-merges. The job waits until the file has finished copying, including from a network path.

Can ReadySetPDF do variable data printing (VDP)?

Yes. Open ODS, XLSX, or CSV. Click a column, then the template; click a box to select it (Ctrl+Z undoes). Address blocks actually place, with bold, italic, and underline. Mail merge prints in millimetres, including tight envelope windows. Output page count is template pages times rows that contain data.

Can ReadySetPDF preview fold types?

Yes. Fold-type previews cover common bindery folds such as letter, tri-fold, gate, and Z-fold. You can export a proof with die lines so the customer and the finishing department are looking at the same fold layout.

Does the booklet maker compensate for spine creep?

Yes. Creep is paper thickness; each inner signature adds that amount. Outward moves content toward the spine; inward opens the gutter. A 3×3 white-space anchor pads margins from the chosen cell, same as Grid.

What preflight checks does ReadySetPDF run?

3.54 checks page size, RGB, images under 250 DPI, and font names. 4.0 is a press-side audit you can save as a recipe: fonts (including form artwork), the DPI you set, color, bleed, hairlines, ink coverage, transparency, overprint, and JPEG 2000. Pass, Fail, and Maybe each get a folder. Free reads the report on screen. Pro exports JSON or PDF and can route the file.

What are the system requirements?

Microsoft Windows. ReadySetPDF 3.54 installs with a setup executable. No browser and no online account.

Does ReadySetPDF work with MediaBox, CropBox, and TrimBox?

Yes. On import you can keep the original boxes, set CropBox to TrimBox, or clip extra margins to TrimBox. Those choices apply to preview, export, and batch. Monkey, Grid, and Booklet can also honor the TrimBox for imposition.

What's coming in ReadySetPDF 4.0?

Press-side preflight saved as recipes, three hot-folder pipelines (including mail merge), and three OCR watch folders. A 30-day trial, then Free or Pro. Hot-fix updates, page geometry in the unit you already use, and panes that pop out. 3.54 remains the current download.
